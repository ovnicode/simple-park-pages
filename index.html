Gameplay

Every lot is a new puzzle

Open car parks, tight rows between parked cars, curbside spots and dim underground garages. Each stage asks for a different line.

Features

Easy to pick up, satisfying to master

19 handcrafted stages

Forward bays, reverse parking, angled spots, curbside parallel parking and underground garages, each one harder than the last.

Real steering-wheel controls

Turn an on-screen wheel and work the pedals, with adjustable steering sensitivity. A short walkthrough teaches you the basics on your first drive.

Parking sensor

Turn on the beeping sensor and its visual distance gauge to see exactly how close you are before you touch a bumper.

Three stars, best times

Park cleanly and beat the target time for three stars. Your best time on every stage is saved, so there is always a record to beat.

Play on phone or PC

Touch controls on Android; on Windows and Linux, drive with the keyboard and rebind every key in Settings.

Offline, in your language

No account and no connection needed. Play in English, French, Spanish, German or Brazilian Portuguese.

Collect all nine badges

64-bit (x86_64). Make the AppImage executable, then run it:

./SimplePark-x86_64.AppImage

Won't start? Install libfuse2, or download the .tar.gz.
