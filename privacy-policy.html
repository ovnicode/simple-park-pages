Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Simple Park is an offline parking puzzle game. The current version does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Game data

Progress, settings, and game-state information are stored locally on your device so the game can remember your progress. This information is not sent to us.

Advertising

Simple Park uses Google AdMob to show a rewarded video ad only when you choose to watch one to refill hearts. AdMob may collect device or advertising identifiers and app activity to deliver and measure ads. We ask for your consent to personalized advertising where required by your region's law (GDPR/CCPA) before any ad is requested.

Children

Simple Park does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If a future version adds online services, analytics, or purchases, this policy will be updated before those features are released.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: support@ovnicode.com
